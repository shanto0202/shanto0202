## About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at the **Bangladesh University of
Engineering and Technology (BUET)** with an interest in software
engineering, applied artificial intelligence, backend systems,
algorithms, and optimization.

I enjoy turning ideas into complete, usable software systems. My work
includes backend APIs, optimization engines, interactive applications,
and projects developed through hackathons, game jams, and technical
challenges.

Currently, I am strengthening my computer science fundamentals while
exploring how modern AI techniques can be integrated into reliable
software systems.

### AI-Assisted Energy Optimization System

**BUP CSE Fest 2026 · Hackathon**

An API that combines natural-language instructions, deterministic
validation, and linear programming to generate optimized energy
schedules.

The system translates operator instructions through an LLM, validates
the resulting directives, applies them as optimization constraints, and
independently verifies the generated schedule.

`LLM Integration` `Linear Programming` `REST API` `Validation` `Docker`
`Testing`
